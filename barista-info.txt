All baristas and floor staff are required to attend the 7:00 AM shift briefing before opening. Please ensure that espresso machines are backflushed and wiped down before the first customer arrives. Inventory counts for milk, syrups, and pastries must be completed by 6:45 AM and logged in the back-of-house spreadsheet. Any equipment malfunctions should be reported to the shift supervisor immediately and documented in the maintenance log. Tip pooling for the morning shift is calculated daily and distributed by the closing manager.
